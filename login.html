Retail Mini-ATM

About Our Concept
Retail Mini-ATM transforms local shops into instant cash access points using UPI. Instead of relying on traditional ATMs—which are expensive to maintain and often out of service— customers simply visit a nearby store, pay using UPI, verify a secure PIN+Code, and collect cash. This model reuses existing retail cash flow to provide a faster, safer, and more accessible alternative to ATMs.

  • Withdraw cash at retail stores using UPI
  • Secure: OTP login + UPI PIN + verification code
  • Reliable: No ATM downtime or maintenance issues
